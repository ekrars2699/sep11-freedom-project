# Entry 6
##### 5/14/26

#### Context
After completing the freedom project we had to present what we had made for the project to the class. For the presentation we made slides that explains what we made, how we made it, and what we learned from working on the project. Later after presentations we had an Expo where everyone shows off their projects to judges and to anyone interested in learning about the projects.

#### Content
In order to engage the audience I needed a hook which was something I had trouble making. I had multiple ideas of what my hook could be like but in the end I decided on having something high energy which was "Do YOU want a challenging game, Do YOU want a game that's simple but ADDICTING, well then you HAVE to play MY game" but it was a generic hook that could've been used for many different games. In the future instead of having just a broad hook I could've made it much more specific perhaps including more of what you do in my game rather than puzzles in general. My takeaway slide was also broad similarly to my hook, while I did have specific takeaways from my project I didn't explain them in enough detail causing them to be more generic. My biggest takeaway from presenting my project is to talk slower. I feel like I was talking too fast because of being nervous and not wanting to use too many filler words such as like or um. Talking too fast caused me to forget what I was going to say and it made me say things incorrectly. I had very little annotations on my slides because I was going to explain my code outloud but due to forgetting some of the things I was going to say I don't think I properly conveyed what my code did causing my audience to be confused. I think in the future I should talk slower as to not make these kind of mistakes, it would be better to present slower rather than not being able to explain what I made. I also should annotate my slides more thoroughly because just having a bit more detail on the slides would allow me to not have to rely on my memory as much which allows for less errors to happen. I do think I did well on projecting my voice and giving my audience eye contact which is something I improved on from last years freedom project presentation, I think as I continue to give presentations I will improve more until I stop making mistakes all together. For the expo we only had 1 minute to present our project to the judges, I had trouble deciding on how to cut my presentation down to just a minute and presenting only the essentials. What I ended up doing was showing how the game worked and the different features as well as briefly explaining what I used to make it while not going into detail about the actual code was in order to save time. In practice and the actual expo I was able to show off my entire game without going over the 1 minute time limit. I think both of these presentations have improved upon my communication and public speaking skills because I had presentations after the one for my freedom project and the expo, during those presentations I was much less nervous and able to properly communicate what those presentations were about.
